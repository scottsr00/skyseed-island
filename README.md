# Skyseed Island
A dependency-free, browser-local pixel-art math adventure for ages 5–6, 7–8, 9–10, and 11–12.

Run `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:5173. Run `npm run check` for JavaScript syntax verification.

- The top energy meter is active. Swimming drains 0.65 energy per second. Riding sharks, marlins, sea dragons, or a swimming pet dragon drains 0.325 energy per second, half the swimming rate. Correct discovery, repeat-practice, or flash answers restore 25 energy, capped at 100. At zero energy the explorer returns safely to land and must solve a problem before swimming or launching a boat again. Saved energy stays with the explorer across age changes, resets, and reloads.
- Full-window browser map with responsive camera proportions, floating crystal/status displays, a left-side rail of flat pixel-art icons, grouped submenus, and nonmodal map panels, and fishing controls over the map.
- Keyboard (WASD/arrows), touch movement, and tappable destination markers.
- 100 guided math challenges per age level (400 total, 20 per destination) across five island destinations.
- Ages 5–6 cover counting with dot pictures, number order, addition/subtraction within 10, comparing numbers, shapes, number bonds, and patterns. Ages 7–8 cover place value, addition/subtraction within 100, the 2/5/10 times tables, equal sharing, time, money, halves/quarters, perimeter, and simple data. Flash arithmetic also adapts to these brackets.
- Five crystals per first completion; repeat practice earns crystals after all 100 discoveries are complete.
- Buy waterskis for 100 crystals or a wakeboard for 25 crystals in Island upgrades or Fishing & travel → Watersports dock. Press E at the Moonwater dock to ride; if you own both, choose which equipment to use. Waterskis appear as twin skis and use the same towing controls. Both purchases save per explorer and age level. The boat automatically follows the center of the island channel to a repeating open-water circuit with over 200 pixels of island clearance. WASD/arrows control the wakeboarder; momentum and stern-mounted rope tension carry the rider through turns. X / Finish wakeboarding animates the rope dropping and the rider settling into the water, then resumes swimming at your current position while the empty boat returns to the dock. Hitting an obstacle releases the rider while the empty boat finishes its programmed route and docks; land crashes use the shark tumble, prone pause, head shake, and get-up animation. The wake stays in world space. Board ownership and safe reload positions are saved.
- Some Mainland Beach walkers carry frisbees. Stand near one and press X to play catch. Move with WASD/arrows, press X to throw or catch a nearby disc, and Esc to leave.
- Restore a cottage (15), garden (20), and bridge (25). Bridge restores a scenic woodland crossing.
- Seventeen islands in a 6,400 × 1,800 world, with twice the previous map area and open ocean on both sides of the centered island group. The original five-island region is preserved. Walkways link the main island, Moonwater, and Sunseed. The former Whispering Island crossing is now a shorter ferry pier, with the ferry berthed in open water on its west side. Swim to Whispering Island or restore the woodland bridge to walk there. Twelve outer islands have individual biomes, boat docks, and practice markers: Palm Cove, Coral Key, Turtle Lagoon, Sunset Atoll, Mango Isle, Starfish Bay, Moonstone Island, Bamboo Retreat, Ember Island, Aurora Cay, Pearl Haven, and Driftwood Isle. They require arrival by your sponsored fishing boat or the ferry; journal entries, swimming, and creature rides do not grant access. Use X near an outer dock to step ashore and E or Climb into boat to reboard. Island visits and the parked boat persist across reloads.
- Mainland Beach spans the southern edge of the map, with colorful umbrellas, sunbathers, strolling visitors, and three volleyball courts. Walk up to any court and press E / Play volleyball to join a doubles match with your selected character and an AI beach teammate against two beach players. WASD/arrows move, Space / Enter / Hit times a return, and X / Escape leaves. A landing ring guides returns, ball arcs and the net affect play, and the first team to five wins. Matches can be replayed and preserve access to your anchored boat. Buy a permanent 50-crystal anchor in Gear; ownership stays with the explorer across all age levels, adventure resets, and reloads, including anchors found in older saves; it works with either the fishing boat or ferry. Within 105 world pixels of the shore, Q or Anchor & go ashore parks the boat and animates the trip ashore. Return to the mooring flag and press E or Return to anchored boat to reboard. Boat position, type, and anchor ownership persist across reloads; ownership also persists across age changes and adventure resets.
- A surf rental shack on Mainland Beach rents a board for 20 crystals until it is returned. Ambient beach swells have a rounded, shaded lip that peels right to left, trails scattered spray and whitewater, then flattens into patchy foam against the mainland shore. Crumbling foam follows island coastlines; water clipping and drawing waves beneath land keep the islands clear. Renting adds catchable crests in the local break. Wave height increases with distance from shore: the outer break at 280 world units retains the original full-size wave, while every nearer wave is smaller. Catching a wave fixes its height for that ride; paddle-out controls show the available size. The face-on riding view uses a broad concave blue wave face with continuous lighting gradients, smooth water contours, a wider hollow barrel with a dark roof and reflected light along its inner wall, and a translucent falling curtain tapering toward a later impact farther right. Fine rivulets, rising white froth ribbons that bend around the breaking lip, subpixel droplets, and irregular foam eddies add surface detail. Froth runs along the whole crest, thickening toward the break, while face ribbons rise rapidly around the rolling surface. The crashing lip has a dense boiling foam core with porous texture and a wider impact plume; its spent whitewater extends to the right edge, tapering as it ages. Crest plumes, falling fingers of whitewater, and dense broken foam at the base follow the supplied surfing screenshot as a visual reference. The surfer flexes knees, shifts weight, and counterbalances with their arms; stronger turns and higher speed generate larger rail spray and a curved wake left along the actual board path. Landings compress the stance and kick up a short burst of spray. E at the shack opens rentals; paddle with WASD/arrows and catch a passing crest with Space/Enter. Riding switches to a face-on scrolling wave: the surfer stays near screen center, travelling left while water streams right, with breaking whitewater chasing from the right. Up/W climbs and Down/S descends; linked up-and-down carves build speed to escape the lip, while coasting loses speed. Ride half inside the barrel curtain for 4× line points (fully under it earns 2×). Press Space at the crest while climbing with speed to launch an air, use left/right for spins, then land with the visible board pointing from straight down (6 o’clock) toward the lower left, excluding horizontal (9 o’clock). Successful airs award points and a speed boost; Down helps align the board. Cutbacks, wave coverage, linked turns, and clean aerial spins also score points. Heats last 90 seconds or three wipeouts; another heat uses the same rental. P pauses, X returns to the beach, and touch buttons mirror the controls. Best scores, rental state, and safe beach positions persist. Controls take inspiration from the [California Games Lynx manual](https://atariage.com/manual_html_page.php?SoftwareID=2413); original procedural wave artwork takes visual inspiration from [Surf Sandbox](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4480760/Surf_Sandbox/).
- A ferry waits at the main island dock. A permanent 50-crystal pass lets you drive it freely with WASD/arrows, using the fishing boat’s movement speed, heading, collision handling, motor response, and wake. F opens fishing from the rear deck, R reels, and X steps onto a nearby dock or dives offshore. A ferry pass permits reboarding without fishing sponsorship. Sail to Crystal Cavern or the outer islands; parked ferry type and heading persist after an outer-island arrival.
- Fishing takes place directly in the main map’s sea, with inline controls and badges beneath the map. Stop fishing or press Escape to resume walking or steering. Fishing dock on the main island: 5 crystals buys a rod and five casts; refill only after bait runs out. Watch for faint underwater silhouettes, partial surfacing, and wakes, then cast ahead. A bending rod and whipping line carry a tiny worm (or lure) through a 0.9-second cast, ending in splash rings. Click/tap to aim, or use arrow keys and cast with Enter/Space. A bite hooks the fish without awarding it yet: tap R repeatedly (or the Reel in button) six times to land it. The fish struggles against a bending rod while the angler leans back and pulls upward. Two seconds without a reel press releases the fish.
- Close/medium/far catches are barracuda, stingray, and shark. A permanent 20-crystal lure unlocks a switchable snook, blue-ring octopus, and blue marlin panel. All six species have saved badges with catch counts per age level.
- Five total catches unlock a Skyseed Angling Co. sponsorship celebration, unlimited bait, and a steerable fishing boat at the main ferry dock. Persistent map hints explain casting and boat controls. X / Exit boat steps onto a nearby dock; offshore, the rider first throws an anchor with an unfurling rope, then dives into the sea. The boat stays still during the throw and the rope remains while it is moored. Anchor throws land in clear water at least 72 pixels from the boat. Reboarding automatically hauls the rope and anchor aboard before steering resumes; beach moorings retain their anchor position across reloads. Swim with WASD/arrows, walk onto shore, or use E beside your parked boat to reboard. Fish from your boat or return it to the dock; Crystal Cavern still needs a ferry ticket. Sponsorship is saved per age level.
- Walk off any island shoreline to dive into the sea with the same animation as a boat dive. Bridges and docks keep walkers on the timber; press X or the Dive button to enter the water explicitly. Ordinary shoreline walking enters the water automatically and does not show a Dive suggestion. No sponsorship is required. Swimmers and swimming mounts pass underneath bridges, docks, and boat hulls, with their bodies and splashes hidden by the structures above. Swim onto land to reach onto the beach and pull yourself up before walking again; Crystal Cavern still requires its ticket. The compact, text-free miniature chart shows all five islands, crossings, docks, your position, and the camera footprint.
- Swim within reach of a surfaced shark to automatically latch on. The shark cruises at a relaxed roughly 1.5 times swimming speed, with gradual turns and an animated tail beat; WASD/arrows steer it around islands. A fading foam wake trails behind the rider. Every ten seconds the shark smoothly submerges and resurfaces, briefly leaving only the rider’s bright snorkel above water; steering continues underwater. All swimmers also wear the snorkel alongside their goggles. X or Hop off shark returns to swimming, with a brief cooldown before another latch. Approaching an unlocked shoreline makes the shark dive offshore while the rider rolls onto walkable beach. Shark rides clear when returning to shore, boarding a boat, or changing profiles/age levels.
- All sea animal rides (shark, blue marlin, and Loch Ness monster) share steering and shoreline avoidance, a ten-second dive cycle, head-first submerging beneath bridges, snorkel and foam wakes, and the same quick shore tumble, prone pause, head shake, and recovery.
- Occasional Loch Ness monster sightings appear briefly in open water, with a tiny wake, two separate humps, and a long-necked head pointing along its direction of travel. A flowing side-to-side wave animates its neck, humps, and tail. Swim within reach to catch a ride; arrows/WASD steer, X or Hop off dismounts, and approaching an unlocked beach rolls the rider ashore as the monster dives. Sightings have a 70% chance at checks every 30–60 seconds of eligible active play, remain temporary, and clear on profile or age changes.
- Blue marlin also surface near swimmers, with a blue back, silver belly, forked tail, and long bill. Swim within reach to ride one; arrows/WASD steer and X or Hop off dismounts. Marlin dive beneath bridges and land riders safely on unlocked beaches. Riding does not award a fishing catch or require a lure.
- A violet pet dragon waits beside the cottage. Press E nearby or tap Fly on pet dragon, then steer with WASD/arrows. It flaps its wings, casts a ground shadow, and flies over land and water. X descends onto clear accessible island ground or splashes down in open water. In water the dragon folds its wings, paddles, and leaves a wake; X takes off again, and swimming toward an unlocked beach climbs ashore. Swimming uses the rider’s energy; Return to cottage brings the rider home. Ferry and boat-only island gates remain in effect. Flight saves use the last safe takeoff point, and the pet returns to the cottage on profile/age changes or reload.
- Sprout hatches after eight discoveries and follows the explorer.
- Squishees unlock every 50 lifetime crystals: 29 treasures across Common, Uncommon, Hard-to-Find, Rare, Ultra Rare, and Mythic tiers. Includes the requested NeeDoh catalog, with the original seven treasures and existing saves preserved. Complete all discoveries to keep earning through repeat practice.
- The stash contains an island trading counter and a six-tier catalog. Earn one trading ticket by answering 10 flash arithmetic questions correctly in a row, with a fresh 15-second deadline for each question. Wrong answers, timeouts, and leaving end the streak; retries are unlimited. Flash questions match the selected age level and award tickets separately from discovery crystals.
- Spend one ticket to swap one owned squishee for another in the same tier. Tickets and inventory save independently per age level. The sole Mythic item has no swap partner until more Mythic items are added.
- Settings contains named explorer profiles with boy/girl character choices and a character preview. Create a profile, rename it, or switch players from the saved profile picker. Characters appear while walking, boating, diving, and swimming. Each profile saves its own selected age level and independent progress for all four age levels, including crystals, collections, trading tickets, upgrades, and fish. Ferry passes stay with the explorer across levels. Existing browser progress belongs to the initial Explorer profile and can be renamed. Profiles use browser localStorage; no account or backend.
- Settings → Admin override accepts password `11111` to add 1,000 spendable crystals to the active explorer’s current age level. The grant saves immediately, requires the password each time, and leaves discovery progress and lifetime earned rewards unchanged.
- Optional synthesized sound. All pixel artwork is original Canvas code.

The journal links to destinations and offers a confirmed progress reset. Save failures show “Session only.” New deployments preserve local progress on the same origin.

## Verification and publication
`npm test` checks all 100 answers, duplicate rewards, fraction equivalence, upgrade costs, the ferry ticket gate and return trips, drawing execution, and walkable destination landing points. Trading tests cover collection migration, all six tiers, ticket rewards, wrong answers, timeouts, same-tier restrictions, inventory persistence, and age separation. Profile tests cover legacy progress, unique names, character selection, reloads, independent age saves, profile switching during activities, and resets limited to the active profile. These are logic tests with a minimal DOM substitute; browser appearance and touch behavior still need live browser verification.

Sites identity is retained in `.openai/hosting.json`. Initial publication was blocked because the environment could not resolve the Sites Git host. Local preview was also blocked by socket permissions and an unavailable browser host. The game can be opened directly from `dist/index.html` or served with the dev command in a normal local terminal.

Ferry passes belong permanently to each explorer profile, across all four age levels, adventure resets, and reloads. Existing passes saved in any age level migrate automatically; new explorers earn their own pass.

The password-protected Settings admin override refills and saves the explorer’s normal energy to 100, and also enables session-only unlimited energy for the selected explorer and age level. The saved refill survives a page refresh. The meter displays “∞ Unlimited,” water activities do not drain saved energy, and Exit admin mode restores normal limits. Changing player or age, resetting the adventure, or reloading ends admin mode. The existing 1,000-crystal grant still requires the password each time.

If a standing explorer is in the pet dragon’s visible fire plume, a small flame flickers in their hair for 0.65 seconds, then fades into rising smoke. The effect follows the head, is cosmetic only, triggers once per breath, pauses for dialogs, and clears on explorer or age changes. Mounted riders are excluded.

- Ferry and fishing boat movement checks the full hull against land, docks, built bridges, and other boats (including the tow boat). Turns and long moves stop before contact; boats can reverse away. Boarding positions provide hull clearance beside the dock.

Boat driving: Up/W applies forward thrust, Down/S brakes before reversing, and Left/A or Right/D steers smoothly relative to the bow. Both vessels accelerate gradually, coast when released, and stop their momentum on collision.

Press Space while driving either boat to trim the twin outboard motors: five separate presses raise trim from 0 to 5, then five lower it back to 0. Trim positions 1–4 widen the spray and add up to 18% forward speed, with position 4 the fastest. At position 5 the props are nearly out of the water: the boat slows to 75% of its untrimmed top speed while throwing larger twin rooster tails. Starting from rest at maximum trim takes about 11 seconds to reach its reduced forward top speed. The maximum-trim rooster tail stays full-sized under forward throttle even from a standstill. Lowering back to 4 restores propulsion; spray fades after releasing the throttle. Holding Space does not repeat trim changes, and Space still casts while fishing.

Both boats display twin outboards with trim-adjusted lower units. Spray uses individual gravity-driven droplets carried by the boat’s motion, with varied launch speeds, fine mist, and small landing splashes.

Outboards swivel together with steering, reverse their angle for reverse thrust, and direct spray with the engines. The ferry carries larger outboards. Boarding either boat starts the motors with a brief exhaust puff and a 0.55-second startup before propulsion engages.

Dragon fire ignites trees touched by its plume, including pines and palms on outer islands. Burning trees keep their original broadleaf, pine, or palm shape, darken, and burn away from the crown down to ash over 4.2 seconds. Their rising flames use the dragon’s layered, rippling fire renderer, including flame tongues and embers. Hit detection follows the dragon’s flight heading and altitude. Tree fires pause for dialogs and reset with the dragon when changing explorers, age levels, or resetting the adventure. After burning down, trees gradually grow from a small sapling back to their original size over five minutes of active play, while the ash fades. Regrowth pauses for dialogs too.

Dragon fire also explodes the ferry, sponsored fishing boat, and watersports tow boat when the plume touches their hull or cabin. The hull stays visible while flames spread for 1.8 seconds, then erupts into a detailed, smoothly shaded fireball with sparks, rolling smoke, and a water pressure ring. Sixty-four recognizable pieces (planks, hull panels, glass, engines, rails, and seats) tumble along gravity-driven trajectories. Each piece checks the shoreline at impact: sea impacts make individual splash crowns and ripples, with wooden wreckage floating briefly and metal sinking; pieces on land bounce, slide, and settle. Wreckage fades and the boat returns after 11.8 seconds. Occupants drop into swimming, and boarding is blocked until the smoke clears. Explosions respect the dragon’s heading and altitude, pause during dialogs, and clear with explorer, age, or adventure resets.

Spraying a beach volleyball player interrupts that court: the sprayed player throws the ball toward your position, then walks or swims to collect it and carries it back before play resumes. Balls that land offshore float with small ripples. Repeated spray does not restart a retrieval; reactions pause during dialogs and clear on explorer, age, or adventure resets. Boat spray passes in front of a nearby dragon when the droplets are above his height, and behind him when he flies above them.

Earn 500 lifetime crystals to unlock the main island’s new speedboat dock, southeast of the original harbour. No crystals are spent. The unlock stays with that explorer across age levels, adventure resets, and reloads; existing saves with 500 earned crystals also qualify. Launch from the dock with E, tap the dock, or use “Launch my speedboat” in Fishing & travel. The teal-and-white speedboat has three outboards with coordinated steering, exhaust, wakes, and trim spray. Its forward top speed is exactly 25% above the fishing boat at every matching trim setting (175 versus 140 at neutral trim; 206.5 versus 165.2 at trim 4). Fishing, anchoring at the beach, outer-island docking, saved parking, reboarding, and dragon-fire explosions work with the speedboat.
